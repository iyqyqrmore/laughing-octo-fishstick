# CoinCat v1.05 — intelligent one-leg portfolio shadow

CoinCat studies whether a native conversion among BTC, ETH, SOL, LINK and ADA can improve risk-adjusted total portfolio USD value after its own entry costs. Its main performance measure is **net modeled USD advantage versus the unchanged original five-asset HOLD portfolio**. Forecasts are unvalidated and modeled maker fills are conditional.

This update adds a separate **Multi-horizon** policy: immediate marked improvement and direct 5m, 15m, 30m, 2h and 24h evaluations. A supported short horizon can qualify while hourly history is incomplete. Unsupported or unattractive alternatives explain HOLD. There is no minimum dollar gain, compulsory return to ETH, reverse-trade fee or mandatory trade cadence.

The existing **24-hour baseline** keeps its trial identity, frozen settings, original quantities, wallet, clock and history. Switching the policy view never starts, resets or migrates a trial. Each independent trial has one shared finite simulated wallet and at most one active order. Independently started live trials are **not a paired performance comparison**; the offline comparison driver uses identical synthetic conditions for HOLD, 24h, multi-horizon and naive momentum.

Start with [Windows installation and acceptance](START_HERE.md), including the procedure preserving an existing v1.05 installation. Manual Spot keeps seven native tabs, 1h defaults, inset transparent logo and private live portfolio hover. Close exits safely; tray/custom minimize behavior and the Windows minimize button remain removed.

Open **Forecast / rejections → History / progress** for per-reference downloads, aligned bars, failures and review states. Separate caches require 3 days of minute bars, 10 days of five-minute bars and 100 days of hourly bars for their respective models. Immediate risk needs 61 closed minutes. Missing/conflicting candles never become fabricated readiness.

**Construct / explain…** accepts a native source budget, such as BTC for an ETH-BTC BUY. CoinCat computes rounded ETH base quantity and its own native fee, separating ticket validity, economics, execution evidence and authority. The reported November 9 fill is an evidence example, not an invented historical recommendation or exact settlement receipt.

Conditional orders are LIMIT GTC Post Only: 30s reviews, 120s assessment, 600s child, 1,800s original campaign and at most three replacements. Reconcile before replacing. Inventory has no required exit. Quotes, candles, touches, time and downtime never fill. Each filled leg pays its own native commission once; unfilled size pays none. Authenticated rates alone do not prove settled fee currency or precision.

First Start defaults to copying fresh available account holdings, excluding holds; no real funds move. Baseline/settings freeze. v1.04 records remain in the archive. The 168-valid-hour clock excludes offline, paused, partial and suspended periods and can finish without a trade. Detailed reports stay private; diagnostics/monitor export fixed labels/counts only.

Actual live block defaults ON. Deliberate OFF plus a manual click retains bounded **real** trading. **Neither shadow policy has automatic real-money authority**, even with manual block OFF. Actual financial records, accounting, permissions/caps, FIFO/tax, security, store/clock and artwork remain protected.
