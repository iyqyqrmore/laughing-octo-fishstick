# CoinCat v95.0 — ETH Growth development preview

Dark spot-style workspace: chart left, ETH engine controls right, research/watch
activity and explicit order/portfolio views below. Exact observation scope is the
seven owner-named ETH/BTC spot products; six-book passes rotate BTC cross-pairs.
ETH is the sole initial funding source. Four direct ETH paper comparisons need
no pre-owned companions; BTC cross-pairs stay observation-only. Basket value
change is a labeled reference, not assumed earnings. Real orders stay blocked.

Optional local monitoring records redacted app health every five seconds, with
bounded minute history. A separate localhost helper provides an authenticated,
read-only browser/status endpoint suitable for private Tailscale Serve. It has
no trading, app-control or code-update channel; see docs/V95_MONITOR.md.

Allocation now shows ETH and selected companions by default. **Show all balances**
reveals other holdings, including small residuals, with their exact native amounts.
Only visibility changes; full-account drafts, funding, exclusions and active
research remain intact. Unapplied edits stay visible until applied.

Replaces the broad dashboard and multi-horizon experiments with one direct-ETH
research flow. ETH plus at most three manually selected companions; explicit
working-ETH proposal; exact immediate taker benchmark versus a patient post-only
maker hypothesis. Futures, bridges, invented listings and account-balance fallback
are excluded. Current authenticated availability must be verified locally.

Six compact manual faders retain exact text input and keyboard operation. Small
original CoinCat title artwork; concise table/readouts; delayed mouse and keyboard
hover explanations. Allocation remains RAM-only. Changes invalidate pending
analysis and require Apply again. STOP retains displayed findings and rejects
queued results. Eight diagnostics sections match this build and redact quantities.

No fake wallet gains: touch/crossing remains uncertain, fees are estimates with
unknown debit currency/exceptions, native dust stays explicit, and no-fill,
partial/unknown and expired observations cannot earn ETH. Full-fill calculations
are conditional scenarios, not observed fills or realized profit.

Retired experiment source/tests and redundant banner sizes are listed in
[docs/V95_RETIRED.md](docs/V95_RETIRED.md). Core financial, recovery, allocation,
transport and cycle regressions remain. Historical module/database names preserve
compatibility; all active product labels/setup/diagnostics identify v95.0.

Protected modules and real-order block unchanged. No commit/push, production
publication, connected account/Windows acceptance or profitability claim.

Follow-up: quote-history candlesticks with time axes, crosshair, interval/range
selectors and browsing to the oldest stored samples. No external history or
volume is fabricated. Coins/research are below the chart; mixer has its own tab.
Readout help opens near the pointer, flips/clamps to visible bounds and scrolls.
Model ETH defaults to full working ETH after carve-outs; custom amounts survive
refresh, Reset restores the latest default. Other defaults remain 0 bps entry,
0.25% target, 24h horizon, 5s cadence and 15 bps taker stress. Three companions.

Continuity hotfix: stable typed per-market research identity; ETH-only funding
binding; reason-coded pause/resume/expiry/reset events in bounded RAM; original
hypothesis clock preserved during temporary data gaps; no touch inference across
outages. Read-only reconnection has monotonic capped retries, stage/status-only
errors, provider backoff and explicit manual-attention states. Staged cancellation
and Tk generations protect STOP; no order/preview/transfer code was added. Freshness
labels distinguish last-good fee/quote evidence from validity at export. Financial
allocation/reconciliation contracts, canonical economics and hard guards unchanged.
