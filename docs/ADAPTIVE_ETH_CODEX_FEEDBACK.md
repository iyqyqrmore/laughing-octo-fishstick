# CoinCat v100.0 — independent technical feedback

## Verdict

The seven approved listed products are mathematically sufficient to study ETH
returns and BTC-funded companion rotation: four ETH links plus three BTC cross
links. Four direct reversals and six triangular routes fit three legs. This says
nothing about actual account eligibility, sized liquidity, fees or net edge.
The six-book rotating collector cannot guarantee a synchronized seven-book frame.
Only a route's fresh dependencies can form a conditional decision. Current cloud
evidence demonstrates software contracts, not available market opportunities.

## Objections and risks

Patient holding avoids an automatic realized sale but cannot improve expected
return merely by waiting. It can hide loss, lock capital and forgo ETH gains;
recovery can fail indefinitely. Measure every open simulated holding against the
original ETH allocation, after conservative exit costs, with exposure duration
and missing-mark coverage. Completed win rate alone is misleading; favorable
conditional cases can coexist with severe underwater holdings. No 15% stop-loss,
loser quota or guaranteed drawdown cap has been chosen.

Post-only queue position, fills and adverse selection are absent from BBO and
midpoint candles. Touched prices are too optimistic as fill evidence. Fee tiers
alone do not verify debit currencies or membership/rebate coverage. A later rebate
is not spendable inventory; never double-count one embedded in a tier rate.
Zero-fee examples in the harness are explicit synthetic arithmetic only.

## Implemented minimum and deferrals

Implemented: pure bounded listed graph, canonical native path estimates, one
shared simulated reservation budget, full exact SIMULATED_ASSUMPTION events,
fixed-horizon conditional source-HOLD comparisons, open drawdown/uncertainty,
read-only app summary, strict diagnostics/monitor v2 and the transparent header.
No app-created hypothetical wallet or automatically fitted predictor. Pure cases
are RAM-only independent counterfactuals; they never settle a wallet or establish
shared simultaneous allocations. Partial-fill resolution and persistence are
intentionally unsupported. Real-account ETH remains a separate optional input,
with no new connected adapter. Missing evidence keeps recoverable ETH UNKNOWN.

This is preferable to a misleading full paper engine that infers fills from
quotes. It also means this build is not yet an unattended predictive experiment:
the offline caller must explicitly register a signal and horizon. The display
NO DEMONSTRATED EDGE is the correct current result, not a failed strategy test.

## Evaluation protocol — recommendation, not an enabled policy

Before observing outcomes, freeze a simple signal rule, size, cost model, horizon,
entry/non-fill assumptions, exclusions and stop/falsification criteria. Start with
coverage/eligibility/cost measurement; then collect 48–72h supervised coverage,
followed by held-out weeks across distinct regimes if integrity is adequate.
Do not tune the rule on that evaluation period or backfill ideal entry/exit prices.

Compare the same original ETH working exposure and matched-horizon source HOLD.
Register every proposal and no-action, missing/deferred quote, non-fill, partial
unknown, expired/missed horizon and adverse result. Keep overlapping experiments
identified as dependent samples. Report uncertainty and regime-stratified results,
latency, transaction-cost/depth stress, waiting time, exposure and distribution of
open plus closed losses; avoid a selectively high win-rate headline. Observation
gaps cannot imply uninterrupted risk coverage. Reject the predictor if held-out
improvement disappears after conservative cost/latency stress, evidence quality
is inadequate or uncertainty dominates. Do not raise allocation/risk to compensate.

## Alternatives and next small patch

- Immediate closed ETH cycles: clean mathematical comparison, but multiple costs,
  residuals and synchronous sized liquidity are substantial barriers. Keep as a
  secondary diagnostic; no automatic cycle admission.
- Patient BTC/ETH maker return: simplest existing observation; collect fee/queue/
  non-fill evidence first. A target is not a forecast or a verified fill.
- Predictive BTC→SOL→ETH: meaningful only if a prospective relative predictor beats
  source HOLD after all costs and fair unknowns. Requires explicit hypothetical
  source inventory and separate forward validation; do not assume a seed amount.
- HOLD ETH: essential primary baseline, zero strategy trading costs, with its own
  market risk. A more complex strategy must demonstrate incremental value.

Recommended next patch: a manually registered, bounded prospective experiment
journal using the existing quote stream and a fixed owner-chosen horizon. It
should preserve signal/outcome identities across restart without storing public
account quantities, include non-events and gaps, and avoid fill inference. This
is a recommendation only; no persistent experiment schema or policy is added here.

## Owner decisions for later work

Choose hypothetical size(s), signal/rule, hold duration and horizon, whether cases
are independent comparisons or jointly reserved exposures, allowed concurrent
paper positions, cost stress and meanings/escalation of a proposed 15% review
alert. Decide how to handle indefinite underwater exposure and opportunity cost.
No real allocation, automatic stop or losing-trade quota is selected by this build.
These choices do not block this source-only delivery.

## Hard blockers to live execution

Verified available/held working funds and external flows; exact fee currency and
settled rebate accounting; complete order/partial-fill settlement; one shared
reservation coordinator; idempotent order submission; restart and unknown-outcome
recovery; tested risk monitoring and safety gates; and explicit future owner
authorization. Unconditional LiveBlocked remains. No live stage is delivered.
