# CoinCat v1.01 — independent technical feedback

The source integration closes the operational gap between an isolated replay
engine and the normal scanner/GUI: one bounded opted-in portfolio can make a
chosen kickoff, process simulated partials/cancels and select a patient return,
conditional rotation or HOLD. All fourteen directions use the seven actual
products, common native accounting and one authority. A tab is not a wallet.

The source-grounded limitation is the current public feed: BBO supplies neither
sized depth nor queue priority. Keeping the inherited collection/request budget
rules out pretending seven full live books and a matching tape were added. The
implemented provider is deliberately a seeded BBO stress scenario. Its invented
queue sizes/probabilities are explicit assumptions, not observed liquidity.
Strict price penetration, delays and haircut partials are more conservative than
a touch-fill shortcut, but they still do not calibrate execution likelihood,
latency, adverse selection or market impact. A realistic observed-liquidity
provider remains future work requiring separately specified data coverage.

Patient holding can lock capital indefinitely, lose more ETH opportunity value,
or fail to recover. Maker-only avoids a taker fallback; it does not remove fees,
losses or unfavorable fills. The adverse synthetic run intentionally retains
underwater BTC while a patient ETH return rests. Midpoint losses stay visible;
realized-paper win rate cannot hide them. No drawdown alert forces a sale or
promises a maximum loss. Idle reservations and non-fills belong in the results.

The default ETH→BTC kickoff is an explicit startup preference, not a prediction.
It does not need a positive multi-leg forecast. Subsequent choices use a frozen
one-step cost threshold with conditional ETH references. Those comparisons may
be useful research controls, but are not a demonstrated predictive signal or
executable arbitrage. Independent journal comparisons never fund this wallet.

Coinbase One Basic confers no assumed Advanced rebate. The other membership
labels also change no rates. Cached authenticated account tiers remain the
admission source; missing/stale/unsupported fees block. Product exceptions and
actual debit currencies are still unverified. Explicit quote-currency PAPER fees
define this simulator's accounting, not the exchange's. Subscription costs are
separately unmodeled. Sized exits, fees and native dust prevent a credible
recoverable ETH number from being manufactured from a midpoint subtotal.

The deterministic seeded fixture and untouched synthetic holdout establish
software and evaluation invariants, not strategy efficacy. No-fill scenarios
must remain in the denominator. Adverse/partial models, unknown marks, missed
horizons and time underwater must remain visible. Evaluation freezes the first
horizon observation inside the 30-second window; later favorable exits cannot
replace it. Any source/model/config changes need a new identified evaluation.

Native per-order asset caps are fixed from the initial ETH slice and each first
canonical modeled acquisition receipt. That is a unit ceiling, not spendable
inventory. Actual known net receipts can differ and leave extra units/dust
outside a later order cap. Source/proceeds of a nonterminal partial stay locked.
This can strand small residues and make whole-portfolio recoverability UNKNOWN;
it is not permission to round sizes up, ignore dust or count it as profit.

Persistence is bounded. Context cache checkpoints do not erase execution events
or spend their IDs; fills/dispatch grants can atomically commit their associated
provider state. A model/storage discrepancy stops for review. Restarts require
exact simulated reconciliation followed by a separate explicit Start; a saved
checkbox cannot seed or resume automatically. Old session databases and losses
are retained and inspectable when a new baseline is explicitly selected.

Before any bounded live pilot: independently verify fee applicability/units,
available/held funds and external flows, realistic queue/trade/depth coverage,
order/partial/cancel reconciliation, latency/outage and restart behavior,
continuous risk limits and held-out strategy performance against the same ETH
HOLD exposure. Native Windows/DPAPI/DPI/single-instance and connected supervised
acceptance are separate evidence. The provided Coinbase references have not
been revalidated through a connected account in this cloud run.

The current result is an operational PAPER research candidate with NO
DEMONSTRATED EDGE. The owner authorized source publication as v1.01 after
verification. That creates no installation, deployment, account change or live
authorization. LiveBroker.submit still raises LiveBlocked immediately, and all
protected broker/FIFO/state/import/key/lot/lock/receipt/transport files stay intact.
